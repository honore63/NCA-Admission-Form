Admission Requirements

Please review the following requirements carefully before completing your application.

📋

Eligibility

  • The applicant must be applying for Nursery One (N1/Baby Class).
  • The child must be at least three (3) years old.
  • Children who are less than three (3) years old, even by one day, are not eligible for admission.
📄

Required Information

  • Child's Full Name, Gender, and Date of Birth.
  • Father's Full Name, National ID, and Telephone.
  • Mother's Full Name, National ID, and Telephone.
  • Residential Address (Province, District, Sector, Cell, Village).
📂

Required Document

  • Upload a clear copy of the child's Birth Certificate issued through IREMBO or National Civil Registration System.
  • Accepted Formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG.
  • Maximum File Size: 100 MB.
  • Maximum Files: 1 Birth Certificate.
